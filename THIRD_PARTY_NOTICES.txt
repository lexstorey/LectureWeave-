Adapted slide-detection concepts
=================================

video-slide-extractor
https://github.com/itsyujay/video-slide-extractor
Reviewed commit: 221454d1cc296e75a2ddf83cd3ed587d5922c232
MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Video2Any

LectureWeave+ slide_detection.py independently translates and modifies the
upstream block-change ratio, activity-mask, footage calibration and settled
dwell concepts for NumPy/OpenCV and the LectureWeave+ timeline data model.

LectureLens
https://github.com/aggarwal-shiv/LectureLens
Reviewed commit: 05b69841bde1555f516892403052ec57ddcaa5e4
MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Shivam Aggarwal

LectureWeave+ slide_detection.py independently adapts the upstream multi-signal
confirmation and perceptual deduplication concepts. Repeated slides remain
separate timeline appearances rather than being deleted.
